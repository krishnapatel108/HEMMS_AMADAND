ℹ️ Operator Checklist Compliance

Under **DGMS (Directorate General of Mines Safety) regulations**, every dumper operator must submit a shift safety checklist before starting machinery.

Our **AI Preventive Maintenance system** instantly joins these human-logged check sheets with **real-time OEM sensor readings**.

*Example:* If an operator marks **"Steering sluggish or stiff"**, the AI cross-references this with the steering accumulator sensor values to isolate accumulator gas leaks, flagging a critical alarm even if the sensor values are just starting to decline.
